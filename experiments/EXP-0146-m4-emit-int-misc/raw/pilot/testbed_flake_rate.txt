DISCLOSURE (2026-08-28, written immediately after PASS 4):
PASS 3 and PASS 4 were executed WHILE `harness/run_sweep.py --run-id run02` was running in the
background on the same GPU. Their sharply higher mismatch rate (up to 22/100 vs 0-6/100 when the
device was otherwise idle) is therefore evidence of a DIFFERENT phenomenon than PASS 1/2:
**concurrent GPU load on this host makes identical, unmutated dispatches return all-zero or
raise contained command-buffer faults at a materially higher rate.**
Consequences, applied for the rest of this experiment:
  1. `run02` overlapped ~2400 of my own extra dispatches and is therefore treated as a
     CONTAMINATED intermediate capture. It is retained (never reused, never edited) and is NOT
     used as a promotion gate.
  2. `run03` is captured as the clean second gated run with nothing else issued from this
     experiment; `run01` + `run03` are the promotion gate.
  3. Every run01/run03 disagreement is adjudicated in `run04` by re-testing that exact case
     5 times, serially.
  4. This host is shared with other concurrently running experiments that this experiment cannot
     observe or control, so a low background rate of spurious zeros/faults CANNOT be excluded
     even for run01/run03. That is exactly why single-observation promotion is refused here.
