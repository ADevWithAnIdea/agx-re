TASK 5 — Color write-mask packing  (FF pool 0x58000+0x194)
=====================================================================
MTLColorWriteMask bit meaning: R=0x8 G=0x4 B=0x2 A=0x1.
0x58000+0x194 (LE u32) per --wmask:
  wm15 RGBA 0x0001ffff | wm8 R 0x00000001 | wm4 G 0x00000002 | wm2 B 0x00000004
  wm14 RGB  0x00000007 | wm12 RG 0x00000003 | wm10 RB 0x00000005
  wm1  A    0x0001fff8 | wm3 BA 0x0001fffc | wm0 none 0x00000000
  => bits[3:0] = write mask, STRAIGHT order R=bit0 G=bit1 B=bit2 A=bit3
     (A18 +0x5c was bit-REVERSED R->bit3..A->bit0 — genuine M5 delta).
     bits[16:4]=0x1fff engage as a block iff the ALPHA channel is written
     (FS store-epilog alpha block; store-class enum co-varies at +0x128:
      0x4c0 when alpha stored / 0x480 when not).
