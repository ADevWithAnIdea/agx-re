## Notes for the reviewer

1. **The headline number is computed, not asserted.** `python3 experiments/EXP-0177-p08-abi-assembly/analysis/isa_status.py` recomputes it from `tools/agx-isa/validation.json`. As of the 2026-08-28 snapshot: 28 stage-ABI instructions considered, 12 emittable (42.9%), 7 of those carrying an `_instruction` label weaker than emitter-grade (EXP-0173 §7.2 showed the emittability rule never reads that label), leaving **5 that clear both bars** — `frame_prologue`, `link_save_restore`, `pixel_order`, `pop_reconverge`, `spill_frame_marker`. **None of the five is an input, output, sysval, interpolation or tilebuffer instruction**; they are all frame/ordering plumbing, and two are themselves compromised (`spill_frame_marker`'s exact role is UNRESOLVED; `link_save_restore` has three zero-free-bit fields). If you want the cell shorter, that sentence is the one to keep.

2. **`validation.json` is a moving target.** EXP-0175 owns `db.json`. If the cell quotes a number it should carry the snapshot date, as the P0.6 cell already does.

3. **The row is genuinely OPEN and should stay OPEN**, but for a different reason than "queued" implies. The honest one-line summary: *the semantics of this row are largely known, almost entirely on the wrong target, and mostly not emittable.*

4. **If you would rather keep the cell short**, the four facts worth preserving above all others are: (a) the 12/28 → 5/28 headline; (b) `get_sr.sr_sel` untested on G17P means no system value can be emitted; (c) `call`'s four `tokenization-only` fields mean the split-epilog contract cannot be built; (d) EXP-0109 and EXP-0117 have no `PROVENANCE.md` rows, so rule 3 fails on the bulk of the evidence.
