# EXP-0199 — pre-registration

**Frozen before any gated run.** Companion machine-readable contract:
`CAPTURE_CONTRACT.json` (source hashes, archive hashes, carrier anchors, raw schema,
timeouts, hang policy, gate).
**Target:** A18 Pro / G17P, `192.168.170.254`. **Clean-room:** OWN-SHADER + HW-PROBE.

---

## 1. The question

Five instructions in `tools/agx-isa/db.json` are **field-complete but blocked at the
instruction level**: every named field is `hardware-run`, yet `validation.json`'s
`_instruction` row is `corpus-correlation` (`frag_depth_store`, `n2_op6`, `vary_slot`) or
`tokenization-only` (`frame_marker_compact`, `sfu_marker`).

That is not a field gap, so **this is not a field sweep.** The open question is whether the
**descriptor itself** — its match bits, its length, and the operation its `semantics`
string names — does what `db.json` claims, **when the instruction is generated by us and
executed on hardware**. EXP-0181's own refresh notes say why the fields cannot settle it:

- `frag_depth_store`: "the one thing the descriptor claims … HAS NEVER BEEN OBSERVED …
  The sweeps were scored against a COLOUR probe."
- `vary_slot`: "Promoting it would certify a slot selector that does not select."
- `frame_marker_compact`: "may not be a standalone instruction at all."
- `sfu_marker`: "Framing right, semantics unknown."
- `n2_op6`: "A bucket has no semantics to confirm."

## 2. Hypotheses, expected observation, and refuter — one per instruction

Each hypothesis is stated so that **"no" is reachable**. Where an arm's conclusion would be
an *inertness* claim, a **detection-power conjunct** is required (FIELD-SWEEP-PROTOCOL
§5a): the observable must be shown moving, on the same arm, for a known-live control.

### H1 — `frag_depth_store` writes the shader `[[depth]]` output to the depth attachment

- **Independent variable:** the six bytes of the instruction at `c_depth` fragment offset
  168 (`d7 14 54 00 00 03`).
- **Observable:** the **Depth32Float attachment**, read back per pixel. Never used to score
  this instruction before.
- **Discriminating oracle:** the carrier writes `depth = v0` (an interpolated gradient) and
  `color.r = 0.5 + 0.25*v0` — *different* per-pixel functions of the same varying. The host
  oracle is therefore `DEPTH == (PIX0.r - 0.5) * 4`, a **three-element vector of three
  distinct values** at the three probe pixels, never a constant. The **colour attachment is
  a paired control**: a mutation of a depth store must move DEPTH and leave PIX0 alone.
- **Confirmed if:** at least one mutation of this instruction's own bytes moves the DEPTH
  surface while leaving the COLOUR surface byte-identical, reproducibly in both runs, and
  the unmutated DEPTH equals the host oracle.
- **Refuted if:** no mutation ever moves DEPTH alone (every mutation moves both surfaces or
  neither), or the baseline DEPTH does not equal the shader's depth output.

### H2 — `vary_slot.slot` (byte+3) selects the varying slot of the following `vary_store`

- **Independent variable:** the four bytes at `c_vary4` vertex offset 28 (`00 0c 40 00`).
- **Observable:** RGBA of the fragment output, which is a direct read-out of four varyings
  whose values are the widely separated constants 1000 / 2000 / 3000 / 4000. Any
  permutation, duplication or dropout is directly readable and names the slot used.
- **Positive control in the same dimension (required):** mutating `vary_store.out_slot`
  (byte+4 of a `57 .. 54 ..`) must move exactly one channel. This proves the carrier and
  the observable *can* express slot selection.
- **Confirmed if:** at least two distinct `slot` values make a channel read a **different
  varying's value**.
- **Refuted if:** no `slot` value ever relocates a varying, while the positive control does.

### H3 — `frame_marker_compact` is a standalone **2-byte** instruction (`60 XX`, byte+2 ≠ 0)

- **Method — independent emission by INSERTION.** We insert the instruction, from bytes we
  generate, at an instruction boundary the compiler did **not** choose, in a straight-line
  compute carrier that contains no `0x60` leader of its own, and shift the tail into the
  container's zero alignment pad (6 bytes available; total file length unchanged). If the
  hardware consumes a different number of bytes than we inserted, the following instruction
  loses its leading bytes and the stream desynchronises.
- **Observable:** the carrier's full 32-lane output against a host oracle, plus an
  integrity sentinel stored *before* the insertion point through its own `device_store`,
  plus 0xDEADBEEF poison in two never-written regions. Four outcomes are separable:
  correct / wrong / halted-early (poison with sentinel present) / never-ran.
- **Confirmed if:** a 2-byte `60 XX` insertion leaves the output exactly correct at ≥2
  boundaries in both runs.
- **Refuted if:** no 2-byte `60 XX` insertion runs correctly at any boundary while the
  4-byte form `60 XX 00 00` does.

### H4 — `sfu_marker` is a standalone **2-byte** instruction (`06 02`)

Same insertion method and same oracle as H3.

- **Confirmed if:** inserting `06 02` leaves the output exactly correct at ≥3 distinct
  boundaries in both runs, **while ≥2 other 2-byte payloads at the same boundaries do
  not** (this second clause is the detection-power conjunct; without it "it ran" would be
  evidence about the method, not about the instruction).
- **Refuted if:** `06 02` insertion breaks the program, **or** every 2-byte payload runs
  clean — in which case the oracle cannot see a broken stream and the arm is void.
- **Match-bit test:** `db.json` claims the family is `(byte0 & 0x07) == 6` and
  `(byte1 & 0x03) == 2`. Both bytes are swept densely in the insertion frame, so the
  hardware's accepted set can be compared against the claimed match.

### H5 — `n2_op6` names an operation whose declared fields control it

- **Independent variable:** the six bytes at `c_depth` fragment offset 48
  (`02 3e 00 00 00 04`), one of three instances in that shader.
- **Confirmed if:** at least one named field, over ≥2 values, yields ≥2 distinct
  non-baseline observations consistent with a documented role.
- **Not established if:** every named field is inert on the observed path while the
  instruction is load-bearing (removing it changes the observation). An inertness reading
  here is reported as *not established*, never as "the fields are inert", because the
  detection-power conjunct only shows the **observable** can move, not that this
  instruction's operands are on the observed path.

## 3. Falsifiers — pre-registered to FAIL. If one passes, the named arm is VOID

| id | falsifier | must produce | if it does not |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | identity splice (write `main` back over itself) | byte-identical to the unspliced baseline | splice machinery unsound → **STOP the whole experiment** |
| F2 | delete 2 bytes at a k_line3 boundary | a non-`ok` outcome | compute oracle has no detection power → H3/H4 VOID |
| F3 | `vary_store.out_slot` mutation | exactly one colour channel moves | vary_slot arm has no detection power in its own dimension → H2 VOID |
| F4 | `frag_depth_store` byte+1 = 0x06 (violates the match `[8,8,20]`) | **not** baseline on both surfaces | depth arm has no detection power → H1 VOID |
| F5 | unspliced health check every 200 cases | the frozen baseline | every case after the divergence is marked CONTAMINATED |
| F6 | insertion of `00 00` and `ff ff` at the H4 boundaries | a non-`ok` outcome | "any 2 bytes are accepted" → H3/H4 VOID |

## 4. Controlled variables and known confounders

- **The tail shift is a confounder of every insertion case.** Controlled three ways:
  (a) an append **into the alignment pad only** (offset = end of `main`), which shifts
  nothing and executes nothing; (b) an insertion at the **last boundary before `stop`**,
  where the shift happens but the payload cannot affect the already-stored result;
  (c) the fact that the *same* shift with *different* payloads gives different outcomes —
  which is the whole design.
- **Archives are built once and frozen by SHA-256**, and both gated runs use the same
  files, so compiler variation cannot enter between runs.
- **Concurrency.** Other agents run on this device unlocked, per FIELD-SWEEP-PROTOCOL §7.
  Mitigations: 0xDEADBEEF poison on every read-back, an integrity sentinel through an
  independent path, the on-disk splice sentinel in both runners, `ERRDOM` recorded on every
  non-OK case, `InnocentVictim` cases retried and segregated, and a two-run cross-check.
  **No result here is treated as a confirmation run on a quiet machine**; where a case needs
  adjudication it is adjudicated offline from the poison and the sentinel (EXP-0160's
  filter), and `RESULTS.md` says which was used.
- **A malformed response is a measurement failure**, excluded from agreement and from
  `values_dispatched`, never scored (DEF-0178-1).

## 5. Coverage

Dense `0..255` for every 8-bit field or match byte named in a hypothesis, at the sites
listed in `CAPTURE_CONTRACT.json`; control sets where a dense sweep would desynchronise the
instruction stream into an unbounded hazard (an instruction's own **byte0**, which selects
its hardware length).

## 6. Hazards known before the run, and excluded

- Inserting the 2-byte word `01 00` at a `k_line` boundary **hung the GPU 5 times out of 5**
  in prefreeze pilot 02. Excluded from the frozen matrix; reported as a hazard result.
- `frame_marker_compact` `b1 ∈ {3,7}` hung the device on four of five carriers in EXP-0172.
  Excluded from the 2-byte-form subset.
- Per-arm hang budget **6**; on exceeding it the arm STOPS and is reported PARTIAL
  (FIELD-SWEEP-PROTOCOL §8).

## 7. What this experiment may NOT conclude

- Nothing about M4/G16G. Every result is labelled `target: G17P`.
- An instruction shown inert in one carrier is **not** shown inert in general; §9's
  four-part criterion for an inert promotion is not met by any arm here and no inert
  promotion is proposed.
- A confirmed *framing* fact (length, standalone status, accepted match set) does not
  license a semantic claim. Where the framing is established and the micro-operation is
  not, the honest label stays at the framing level.
