# `work/` — reproducible scratch, deliberately not committed as evidence

* `pilot_locate.json` — the **locate-only pilot** output: for each of our own carriers, the
  compiled `_agc.main` hex and its tokenization on G17P. No GPU dispatch happened to produce
  it. This is the record that every anchor in `CAPTURE_CONTRACT.json` was found, not assumed.
* `cvar/` — the variant COPIES of `tools/agx-isa/` used for the db-change A/B. **Deleted
  after the A/B**, because they are three full copies of the tool tree and are rebuilt
  exactly by `python3 analysis/make_variant.py {pixel_order,vary_store,both}`. The
  measurements themselves are preserved in `analysis/ab/metrics.json`.

Serialized Metal binary archives produced on the target (`work/bin/`, `work/pilot/*.bin`,
the per-request splice archives) live **on the neo only** and are never copied into this
repo: they are binary, they are regenerated by `shdump` from our own MSL in seconds, and
`raw/` is text-only append-only evidence by policy (`SUBAGENT_BRIEF.md`).
