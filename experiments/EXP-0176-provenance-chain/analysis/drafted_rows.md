**These are drafts for the orchestrator's review. `PROVENANCE.md` was NOT edited by this
experiment.** Paste order is up to you; the ranking below is by *exposure*, not by paste order.

## How these were written

- Every claim was read from the experiment's own committed `RESULTS.md` / `report.md` /
  `QUARANTINE.md` / `STOP.md` / `SUPERSEDED.md` / `PROGRESS.md`. Nothing is inferred from a
  directory name.
- **Where an experiment's own text makes a claim its committed artifacts do not support, the row
  says what IS supported and the gap is flagged** in the `⚠ ORCHESTRATOR NOTE` under the row.
  Rows are deliberately *not* inflated: many of these experiments' most valuable output is a
  refusal to promote, and every such refusal is stated in the row itself.
- **Target is named explicitly in every row and never generalized.** Two rows correct a naming
  trap: `EXP-M4-05` and `EXP-M4-06` ran on the **A18 Pro / G17P**, not on the M4, despite the
  `EXP-M4-*` prefix.
- Every cited path was checked to exist (`analysis/cite_paths.py`); every commit hash was
  resolved with `git log --reverse` over the experiment directory.
- **Date column** = the date of the commit that first added the directory. For a few experiments
  the internal run ids carry a later date (e.g. `EXP-0106`'s runs are `m4-20260830-*`); adjust if
  you prefer the observation date.
- No bare `|` appears inside any cell (see `broken_rows.md` — three existing rows are broken that
  way, and one has a markdown heading glued into it).
