  Indirect: config @+0x00 = 0x00880000; a SECOND 0x2c record follows the first (no 0x40000000 terminator

    ptr into arg-buffer 0x100000e0000+0x14c0). The indirect-args buffer VA (0x1000001c900) is staged in

     buffer and converts threadgroups*threadsPerThreadgroup into the thread-count grid. Aux shader is
     Metal-generated (located, NOT disassembled). threadsPerThreadgroup stays inline (tg.x@+0x1c=32).
